The problem

Every red build forces a bet nobody prices.

  • Flaky tests burn reruns — CI minutes and engineer attention on failures that aren't real.
  • They erode trust in CI — once red stops meaning "broken", people stop looking.
  • The costly mistake is asymmetric — quarantining a real bug ships it.

Ask a frontier LLM and you get a verdict

"This test is flaky."
Confident. No uncertainty. No threshold to tune.

Ask Drex and you get a probability

P(flaky) = 0.074 (case_007, a real race bug whose retry passed)
Calibrated. You choose where to act.

Why it matters

  • All 24 real regressions scored P(flaky) ≤ 0.35; 34 of 36 flaky scored ≥ 0.93; 0 of 60 landed in the 0.40–0.80 retry band.
  • A “retry passed ⇒ flaky” rule quarantined 2 real race bugs; Drex blocked both (P = 0.07, 0.09). Frontier LLMs hedged with RETRY on 41 of 60.
  • 58 of 60 scored < 0.1 or > 0.9: handled automatically. A wrong quarantine costs 20× a rerun.

How it works

Extract → score → decide → learn.

1 · Input

CI failure log

Raw test output, stack trace, retry history.

→
2 · NDI

Structured fields

Error type, test name, timing, infra signals, past pass/fail.

→
3 · Drex

P(flaky)

State + typed question in, calibrated probability out. No prose.

→
4 · Policy

Two thresholds

Turn a probability into an action, priced by a cost table.

QUARANTINEP(flaky) ≥ 0.80
RETRY0.40 ≤ P(flaky) < 0.80
BLOCK + ALERTP(flaky) < 0.40
↺ 5 · Feedback: human corrections refit both thresholds against the cost table. The thresholds only move when moving them lowers total cost. Run by Intern, the Nace harness. Qwen3.8-27B on DeepInfra did the work and Drex was System 1, supervising every turn: 3 cases, 15 triage commands, 16 Drex checks, 6 turns, 39 s, and it flagged the one Drex-vs-human disagreement (case_049).

Takeaway

A verdict can't learn your costs. A probability can.

Why probabilities > verdicts
  • Thresholds are a business decision, not a prompt.
  • Uncertainty routes the hard cases to a human.
  • Corrections move a number, not a prompt rewrite.
What's next
  • Run as a CI check on real pipelines.
  • Per-repo cost tables and thresholds.
  • Same loop for other triage calls: alerts, PR risk, incident paging.
The ask
  • Pilot it on one team's CI.
